Are legends born or made?

League Level

Football players in league 1 are considered the best of the best. This league includes players such as Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo. To be at the top of their game, players must possess exceptional skills and dedication. This gets reflected in their performance metrics across various skills, such as pace, shooting, and passing.

Best Players

It appears the best players of football are often between the ages of 25-30 and are in league 1. This is because players in this group have typically reached their physical peak and have accumulated enough experience to perform at a high level. However, there are always exceptions to this trend, with new players always emerging from the lower leagues and older players maintaining their performance levels.

Is Age Just a Number?

Most footballers retire between the ages of 32 to 36. But does age really determine a player's performance? While it's true that physical attributes may decline with age, many players maintain high performance levels well into their 30s. Experience, game intelligence, and adaptability often allow older players to continue excelling on the field, challenging the notion that age is the sole determinant of a footballer's success. Revealing that even older players can have high potential when it comes to football.
